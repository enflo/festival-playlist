Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember information about your visit.

How We Use Cookies

Festival Playlist uses only essential cookies that are strictly necessary for the application to function. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies.

Session Cookie

We use a single signed session cookie to maintain your login state after you authenticate with Spotify. This cookie stores:

  • Your Spotify access and refresh tokens (encrypted)
  • Your Spotify display name
  • An OAuth state parameter for security

This cookie is essential for the application to work and is deleted when you log out or close your browser session.

Third-Party Cookies

When you log in via Spotify, Spotify may set its own cookies on its domain during the OAuth authorization flow. Please refer to Spotify's Cookie Policy for details.

Managing Cookies

You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, but this will prevent you from logging in and using Festival Playlist. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.
