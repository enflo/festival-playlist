Festival Playlist

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Overview

Festival Playlist is a web application that creates Spotify playlists from festival lineup poster images. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Spotify Account Data

When you log in with Spotify, we receive and temporarily store in your session:

  • Your Spotify user ID and display name
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token to interact with the Spotify API on your behalf

This data is stored in a server-side signed session cookie and is not persisted to any database. It is removed when you log out or when your session expires.

Uploaded Images

When you upload a festival lineup poster, the image is processed in memory to extract artist names. Uploaded images are not stored on our servers. They are sent to a third-party AI vision service for text extraction and are discarded immediately after processing.

Playlist Data

Playlists are created directly in your Spotify account via the Spotify API. We do not store copies of your playlists or listening data.

Third-Party Services

Festival Playlist relies on the following third-party services:

  • Spotify API — for authentication, track search, and playlist creation. Subject to Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • AI Vision Service — for extracting artist names from uploaded images. The image is sent to the configured AI provider for processing. No personal information is included in these requests.

Data Retention

We do not operate a database. All session data exists only in your browser cookie for the duration of your session. We retain no personal data after you log out or your session ends.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Log out at any time to clear your session data
  • Revoke access to Festival Playlist from your Spotify account settings
  • Delete playlists created by Festival Playlist directly from your Spotify account

Security

Session cookies are cryptographically signed to prevent tampering. Communication with Spotify and AI services is conducted over HTTPS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
